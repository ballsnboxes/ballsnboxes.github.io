Balls & Boxes

A neon tilt-control arcade

You are a glowing ball in a neon microworld. Tilt your phone and the world rolls you wherever gravity pulls. Escape predatory crystals, blast them away and rescue your own before none are left.

About the game

Tilt to play

No joysticks: you tilt the world itself and the ball rolls across it. You feel the physics in your hands.

Blast, dash and shield

A blast scatters every enemy around you, a tap dashes you away, and a charged dash wraps you in a shield.

37 levels

Traps, energy doors, colour locks, live walls and brittle walls, with a core boss at the end.

Medals

Finishing a level is only the start: there are medals for speed and for taking no damage.

Not a single sprite

All graphics and all sound are made in code: neon nebulae, particles, lightning and shockwaves.

Plays offline

Ads are optional: a video you can watch to revive your ball after a defeat.
